Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

This site does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no forms, no tracking and no adverts. It does not use cookies.

What is stored on your device

Your browser remembers a few settings so they stay the same next time, such as your colour theme, projector mode and course filter. These are kept in your browser's own storage (local storage) on your device. They are never sent anywhere and contain nothing personal. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

The site also saves a copy of its own pages in your browser, so it keeps working if the internet connection drops. Again, this stays on your device.

Links copied with "Copy link" store a simulation's set-up (such as a circuit) in the web address itself. Nothing is saved on a server.

Services this site relies on

Like every website, pages have to be delivered over the internet. Two outside services do this, and they can see the basic technical details every web connection involves (such as your IP address and browser type):

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep server logs for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the text fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google's servers. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

This site doesn't receive, see or store any of that information.

Children

The site is designed for school students. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing children need to provide, and nothing to share with anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if accounts or saving work online are added), this policy will be updated before that happens, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub.
