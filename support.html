If your department finds it useful, here’s how to get more from it, and how to help keep it going.

Questions

Do students have to log in or pay?

No. Students don’t need an account and never pay for anything. Supporting the site doesn’t unlock anything extra for them: it helps keep the site going.

Can my school pay by invoice?

Yes. Training, custom work and supporting-school contributions are all invoiced, so they can go through your school’s usual purchasing.

How much does it cost?

Get in touch with what you have in mind, and you’ll get a price before anything is agreed.

Is the site linked to AQA?

No. The tools follow the AQA GCSE Physics and Combined Science specifications and the KS3 curriculum, but the site is independent. It isn’t produced or endorsed by AQA.
