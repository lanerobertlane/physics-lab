mc | particles | Density | Why does ice float on water? {both} | *Ice is less dense than water; Ice is solid; Ice is lighter than water; Ice is colder than water | EXP: An object floats if it is less dense than the liquid. Ice is unusual: it is less dense than liquid water.
mc | particles | States of matter | Which state of matter has a fixed shape and a fixed volume? {both} | *Solid; Liquid; Gas
open | particles | States of matter | Explain how the energy of the particles changes when the temperature of a substance goes up. | MODEL: The particles gain kinetic energy and move (or vibrate) faster. Temperature goes up because the average kinetic energy of the particles goes up, so the internal energy goes up.
open | particles | States of matter | Explain how the energy of the particles changes when a substance changes state. | MODEL: The energy goes into breaking (or weakening) the bonds between the particles, so their potential energy changes, not their kinetic energy. The temperature stays the same while the state changes, but the internal energy still goes up.
