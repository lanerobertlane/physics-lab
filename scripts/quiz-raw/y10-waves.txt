open | energy | Thermal insulation | Describe how a building can be adapted to lose less energy. {both} | MODEL: Any of: loft insulation, cavity wall insulation, double or triple glazing, draught excluders, thicker walls or walls with a lower thermal conductivity. These reduce the rate of energy transfer to the surroundings.
open | energy | Efficiency | Describe the energy transfers in a light bulb. {both} | MODEL: Energy is transferred electrically to the bulb. The useful output is light. Some energy is wasted, dissipated to the thermal store of the surroundings.
