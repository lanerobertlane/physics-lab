## Gas pressure: balloons
mc | particles | Gas pressure | Why does a balloon get bigger when you blow more air into it? {both} | *More particles hit the inside of the rubber every second, so the pressure inside increases; The particles inside get bigger; The air outside is sucked in; The rubber gets warmer | EXP: More particles means more collisions with the rubber every second. The pressure inside goes up and pushes the rubber out until it balances the push from outside.
mc | particles | Gas pressure | A blown-up balloon is put into a freezer. What happens to it? {ks3} | *It gets smaller, because the particles inside move more slowly; It gets bigger, because cold air expands; It stays the same size; It bursts | EXP: Colder particles move more slowly, so they hit the rubber less hard and less often. The pressure inside falls and the balloon shrinks.
