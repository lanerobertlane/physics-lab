tf | particles | Brownian motion & diffusion | A drop of ink spreads out faster in hot water than in cold water. {ks3} | TRUE | EXP: The particles move faster in hot water, so diffusion is faster.

open | particles | Brownian motion & diffusion | Someone opens a bottle of perfume at the front of a classroom. They spread out from where there are lots of them (near the bottle) to where there are fewer (the rest of the room), so some reach the back. This is diffusion.
open | particles | Brownian motion & diffusion | Each particle moves in a random direction. ## Power: comparing two motors

Which is true? {both} | *Motor A has the greater power; Motor B has the greater power; They have the same power; Motor B transfers more energy | EXP: They transfer the same energy, but A does it in less time, so A transfers energy at a faster rate.

Which has the greater power? {ks4} | *Motor X; Motor Y; They have the same power | EXP: X: 400 ÷ 10 = 40 W. Y: 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W. Power is the energy transferred every second, not the total energy.
open | energy | Power: comparing two motors | Describe how you could compare the power of two electric motors. {ks4} | MODEL: Use each motor to lift the same load through the same measured height, and time each one with a stopwatch.
