open | energy | Energy stores | Give an example of a chemical energy store. | MODEL: Food, fuels (such as petrol, coal or wood) or batteries.
open | energy | Energy stores | What is the law of conservation of energy? | MODEL: Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only be transferred usefully, stored or dissipated.
open | electricity | Circuit builder | Describe the difference between series and parallel circuits. | MODEL: In a series circuit the components are in one loop, so the current is the same everywhere and the p.d. is shared. In a parallel circuit there are separate branches, so the current splits and each branch has the same p.d. as the supply.
tf | electricity | Static electricity & electric fields | Electric charge can flow easily through insulators. | FALSE | EXP: Electrons cannot move freely through insulators, so they don't conduct. This is why charge can build up on them.

## Renewable Energy
open | energy | Energy resources | Name three fossil fuels. | MODEL: Coal, oil and natural gas.
mc | energy | Energy resources | What is a major disadvantage of using non-renewable energy resources? | They are always available; They are cost-effective; They are easy to transport; *They contribute to pollution and climate change | EXP: Burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide, which contributes to climate change, and other gases that cause air pollution.
tf | forces | Pressure in fluids & upthrust | Pressure in a liquid increases with depth. | TRUE | EXP: The deeper you go, the greater the weight of liquid above pushing down.
tf | forces | Pressure on solids | Increasing the area over which a force is applied increases the pressure. | FALSE | EXP: Pressure = force ÷ area, so a bigger area gives a smaller pressure.
mc | waves | Transverse & longitudinal waves | Which property of a wave is measured in hertz? | Amplitude; *Frequency; Wavelength; Speed | EXP: Frequency is the number of waves passing a point each second.
open | energy | Energy resources | Explain why nuclear power is a non-renewable energy resource. | MODEL: Its fuel (uranium or plutonium) is mined and will run out. It is not replaced as fast as we use it.
