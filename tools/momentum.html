Momentum and collisions

Momentum is mass × velocity (p = m v), so it has a direction. Set up two gliders on an air track and watch what happens to the total momentum when they collide, or when they push apart in an explosion. In a crash, see how a longer stopping time means a smaller force.

The crash

A crumple zone squashes slowly, so the car takes longer to stop. Air bags, seat belts, cycle helmets and crash mats work in the same way.
