Energy resources

Renewable resources are being (or can be) replenished as they are used. Non-renewable resources will run out. Each resource has advantages and disadvantages: how reliable it is, the pollution it causes, and its cost.

How did you do?

Nuclear, wind and solar can’t be turned up and down to follow demand. Gas, coal and hydro-electric power stations are switched on to fill the gap, in that order: hydro first, then gas, then coal.
