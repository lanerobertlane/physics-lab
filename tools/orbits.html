Orbits

Launch a satellite sideways from above the planet. Too slow and it falls back; at just the right speed it goes round in a circle; faster and the orbit stretches out, until it escapes.

Remember

  • Gravity provides the force that keeps planets, moons and satellites in orbit. It always pulls towards the centre.
  • In a circular orbit the speed stays the same but the direction keeps changing, so the velocity changes: the object is accelerating towards the centre.
  • For a stable orbit, a smaller radius needs a higher speed.
