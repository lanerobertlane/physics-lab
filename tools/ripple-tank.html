Ripple tank

A view from above of water waves in a tank. Use the ruler to measure the wavelength, and the stopwatch to count waves and find the frequency. Then work out the wave speed with v = f λ.

Required practical: method

  1. Wavelength: freeze the waves. Line the ruler up and measure across as many waves as you can (say 10), then divide by the number of waves.
  2. Frequency: start the stopwatch and count how many waves pass the red line in 10 seconds. Divide the number of waves by the time.
  3. Wave speed: v = f × λ. Change the frequency and repeat.
