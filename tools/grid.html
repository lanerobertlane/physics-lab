The National Grid

Electricity travels from power stations to homes through long cables. Choose the p.d. in the cables and watch what happens to the current, and to the energy wasted heating the cables.

P.d. in the cables:

Why transmit at a high p.d.?

The power sent is the same at every p.d. (P = V I), so a higher p.d. means a smaller current. The heating in the cables depends on the current squared (P = I² R), so a smaller current wastes much less energy. The cables here have a resistance of 0.5 Ω.
