Reading the graphs

  • Distance–time: the gradient (slope) is the speed. A flat line means stopped; a steeper line means faster; a curve means the speed is changing.
  • Velocity–time: the gradient is the acceleration. A flat line means constant speed; sloping down means slowing down.
  • The area under a velocity–time graph is the distance travelled Higher
  • a = Δv ÷ t  ·  distance = speed × time
