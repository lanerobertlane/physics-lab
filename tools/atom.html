Atom builder

Add protons, neutrons and electrons. The number of protons decides which element it is; neutrons make different isotopes; electrons that don't match the protons make an ion.

Particles

Remember

  • Proton: charge +1, mass 1. Neutron: charge 0, mass 1. Electron: charge −1, mass very small.
  • An atom has a radius of about 1 × 10−10 m. The radius of the nucleus is less than 1/10 000 of that (here it's drawn much bigger).
  • An atom has no overall charge: protons = electrons.
  • Electrons can move to a higher energy level by absorbing electromagnetic radiation, and drop back by emitting it.
