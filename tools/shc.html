Specific heat capacity

Heat a 1 kg block with an electric heater. Record the energy supplied (from the joulemeter) and the temperature, then work out the specific heat capacity with c = ΔE ÷ (m Δθ).

Method

  1. Measure the mass of the block (1 kg here) and its starting temperature.
  2. Switch on the heater. Every minute, record the energy on the joulemeter and the temperature.
  3. Work out the change in energy (ΔE) and the change in temperature (Δθ) since the start. Then c = ΔE ÷ (m Δθ).
  4. Try it with and without insulation. Without it, energy is lost to the surroundings, so your value of c comes out too high.
