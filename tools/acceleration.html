Required practical: acceleration

A falling mass pulls a trolley along a runway. Two light gates measure the trolley's speed. Investigate how the force, and the mass, affect the acceleration.

Method

  1. Changing the force: start with all the 100 g masses on the trolley. Move them to the hanger one at a time, so the total mass stays the same. Force = weight of the hanging masses.
  2. Changing the mass: keep the hanging mass the same and add masses to the trolley.
  3. Each light gate measures how long the card (5 cm long) blocks it, giving a speed. Acceleration = change in speed ÷ time between the gates: a = (v − u) ÷ t.
  4. The runway is tilted slightly so friction is balanced (compensated). Untick to see what friction does.
