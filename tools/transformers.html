Transformers

A transformer changes the size of an alternating p.d. An alternating current in the primary coil makes a changing magnetic field in the iron core. This induces an alternating p.d. in the secondary coil. More turns on the secondary coil means a bigger p.d.

Working it out

Transformers only work with a.c. A direct current makes a steady magnetic field, which doesn’t induce a p.d. in the secondary coil. The field has to keep changing.

Real transformers are very efficient (often over 95%), so we usually assume 100%: the power out equals the power in.
