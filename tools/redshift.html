Evidence for the Big Bang

  • Light from almost every galaxy is red-shifted: they are all moving away from us.
  • The further away a galaxy is, the bigger its red-shift, so the faster it is moving away.
  • This means the universe is expanding. Wind it back and it all started from a very small, extremely hot and dense region: the Big Bang.
  • Since 1998, observations of supernovae suggest distant galaxies are moving away faster and faster. There is still much we don't understand, such as dark mass and dark energy.
