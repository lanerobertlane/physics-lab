Efficiency

No device transfers all of its input energy usefully: some is always wasted, usually by heating the surroundings. Efficiency tells you what fraction of the input is useful. It is never more than 1 (100%).

How to do it

  1. Find the total input (everything going in).
  2. Find the useful output (only the part you want).
  3. Divide: useful ÷ total. The answer is a decimal less than 1.
  4. For a percentage, multiply by 100.

efficiency = useful output energy transfer ÷ total input energy transfer

efficiency = useful power output ÷ total power input

Wasted energy = total input − useful output. It is usually dissipated (spread out) to the surroundings as thermal energy.

Check your answer: if you get more than 1, you have divided the wrong way round.

Rearranging

efficiency = useful ÷ total

useful = efficiency × total

total = useful ÷ efficiency

Use the efficiency as a decimal in these: 25% = 0.25.
