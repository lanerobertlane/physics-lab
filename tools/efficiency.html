Efficiency

No device turns all of its input energy into the useful kind. Pick a device to see where its energy goes, then use the calculator for your own questions.

Use energy (J) or power (W) — just use the same for both. Efficiency can be a decimal (0.25) or a percentage (25%).

Remember

  • efficiency = useful output energy transfer ÷ total input energy transfer
  • efficiency = useful power output ÷ total power input
  • Wasted energy is usually dissipated to the surroundings as thermal energy, so it spreads out and is less useful.
  • Efficiency is never more than 1 (100%): energy can't be created.
