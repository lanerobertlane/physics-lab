Circuit builder

Pick a component, then tap the gap between two dots to place it. Drag with the wire tool to draw wires quickly. Tap a component to see its readings or change it.

Remember

  • Series: the same current flows through each component. The potential differences add up to the supply p.d.
  • Parallel: each branch has the same p.d. as the supply. The branch currents add up to the total current.
  • Ammeters go in series, voltmeters go in parallel (across the component).
  • V = I R  ·  P = V I  ·  P = I² R  ·  Q = I t
