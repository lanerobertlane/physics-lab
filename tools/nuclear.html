Nuclear equations

When an unstable nucleus decays it gives out alpha, beta or gamma radiation, and may turn into a different element. In a nuclear equation the mass numbers (top) and the atomic numbers (bottom) must add up to the same on both sides.

The decay plays once the equation is right.

What each decay does

Remember

The mass number (top) is the number of protons + neutrons. The atomic number (bottom) is the number of protons, and it decides which element it is.

An electron isn’t a nucleon, so its mass number is 0. Its charge is −1, so it is written with −1 at the bottom.
