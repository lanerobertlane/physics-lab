Reaction time test

Your reaction time is how long you take to respond to something. It varies from person to person, and is typically between 0.2 s and 0.9 s. Measure your own, then find out what a distraction does to it.

While you drive:

The ruler falls just as fast as a real one. The further it falls before you catch it, the longer your reaction time.
