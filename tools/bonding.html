Bonding

Choose two elements to bond. A metal and a non-metal make ionic bonds (electrons are transferred), two non-metals make covalent bonds (electrons are shared), and a metal on its own has metallic bonding (delocalised electrons).

Tips

Metals lose electrons and form positive ions. Non-metals gain electrons and form negative ions.

Group 1 → 1+, group 2 → 2+, group 3 → 3+. Group 7 → 1−, group 6 → 2−, group 5 → 3−.

The charges in a compound must add up to zero.

In covalent bonds, each shared pair is one electron from each atom.
