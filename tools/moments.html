Moments

Choose a weight, then tap a hook on the beam to hang it there. Tap a weight to take it off. The beam balances when the clockwise and anticlockwise moments are equal.

Moments about the pivot

Remember

  • A moment is the turning effect of a force: M = F d, where d is the perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of the force.
  • Principle of moments: when an object is balanced, the total clockwise moment equals the total anticlockwise moment.
  • A small force a long way from the pivot can balance a big force close to it. That's how levers make jobs easier.
