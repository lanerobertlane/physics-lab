Half-life

Radioactive decay is random: you can't predict which nucleus will decay next. But with lots of nuclei, the number left halves in the same time, again and again. That time is the half-life.

The half-life is hidden. Press Start, then use the graph: how long does the number of nuclei take to halve?

Purple nuclei are unstable and haven't decayed yet. Grey ones have decayed. Each one has the same chance of decaying every second.
