Quick quizzes

Short quizzes that mark themselves. Tap a quiz’s title to see its questions first. For revision on your own, press Try it. To use one in a lesson, press Show on board and the class scans the QR code with their iPad cameras. Every answer is marked straight away, with the correct answer and a short explanation. Your answers stay on your own device and aren’t sent to anyone.

Build a quiz by topic

Options

Answer options are always shuffled. At the end you can redo the ones you got wrong.
