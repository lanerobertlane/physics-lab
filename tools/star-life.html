The life cycle of a star

Every star begins as a cloud of dust and gas. What happens at the end depends on how much mass the star has.

Key ideas

  • Stars release energy by nuclear fusion, joining light nuclei to make heavier ones.
  • In the main sequence, the inward pull of gravity is balanced by the outward pressure from fusion energy, so the star is stable.
  • Fusion inside stars makes elements up to iron. Elements heavier than iron are made in a supernova, and the explosion spreads them through the universe.
