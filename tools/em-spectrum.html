Band widths are not to scale — the real spectrum spans more than 15 powers of ten. The wave drawing is stretched so you can see the change.

True for all electromagnetic waves

  • They are transverse waves.
  • They all travel at the same speed in a vacuum (or air): 3 × 108 m/s.
  • They all obey the wave equation v = f λ. Because v is fixed, a higher frequency means a shorter wavelength.
  • They transfer energy from a source to an absorber.
  • They form a continuous spectrum — the regions blend into each other.
  • Our eyes can only detect visible light, a very small part of the spectrum.
