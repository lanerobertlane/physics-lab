Sound on an oscilloscope

Sounds are made by things vibrating. A microphone turns a sound into a trace on an oscilloscope, so you can see the vibrations. Change the frequency and amplitude, listen to the difference, and read the period and frequency from the screen.

Keep your volume low. If you can’t hear anything, check the sound isn’t muted on this device. Small speakers can’t play the very lowest notes.

The sound on its way to the microphone

Who can hear it?

Why is there a limit? Sound makes your ear drum and the small parts behind it vibrate. These only pass on vibrations over a limited range of frequencies, so normal human hearing runs from 20 Hz to 20 kHz. The top of your range drops as you get older. Sound above 20 kHz is called ultrasound.
